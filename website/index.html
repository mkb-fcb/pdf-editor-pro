PDF to e-book

Small print? Read it as a book.

A PDF page has a fixed layout, so on an e-reader or phone the text stays tiny. As an e-book, the text flows to fit the screen and you choose the font size.

→
1

Open the PDF

Any PDF that contains text: books, reports, articles.

2

Convert → EPUB or Send to Kindle

Chapters, headings, images, charts and tables are kept; page numbers and running headers are removed.

3

Read anywhere

On Kindle, Kobo, Apple Books, Google Play Books or any EPUB reader.

FormatBest forNote

Scanned PDFs (photos of pages) have no text to reflow; their pages are kept as images.
