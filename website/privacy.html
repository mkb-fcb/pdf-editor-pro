Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MKBSoft ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with PDF Editor Pro, a Windows desktop application.

1. No Data Collection

PDF Editor Pro processes all PDF files locally on your computer. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any of your documents, files, or personal data.

Your files never leave your device. No internet connection is required to use the application.

2. License Verification

When you activate a purchased license, the application sends your license key and a machine identifier to our license server (license.mkbsoft.net) solely to verify your license. This data is not shared with third parties and is used only for license validation purposes.

3. No Analytics or Tracking

PDF Editor Pro does not include any analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect usage data of any kind.

4. Purchase Information

If you purchase PDF Editor Pro through Gumroad or the Microsoft Store, payment processing is handled entirely by those platforms. We receive only your email address to deliver your license key. We do not store payment card information.
