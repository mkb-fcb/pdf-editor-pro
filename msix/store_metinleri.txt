--- Product name ---
PDF Editor Pro - MKBSoft

--- Description ---
PDF Editor Pro is a fast, lightweight PDF editor for Windows. Everything runs locally on your PC — your documents are never uploaded to the cloud.

EDIT
• Edit, move and delete existing text directly on the page
• Add new text, images, rectangles and circles
• Highlight content, add stamps and watermarks
• Erase selected areas — the underlying text is permanently removed

ORGANIZE
• Merge multiple PDFs into one
• Split by page range or extract pages
• Rotate, reorder, insert and delete pages with thumbnail view

CONVERT & PROTECT
• Export to Word (DOCX), images, plain text and e-book (EPUB) — and send to Kindle
• Compress PDFs with selectable quality to reduce file size
• Add or remove password protection

Multilingual interface: English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Russian.

FREE DEMO & FULL VERSION
The app is free to download and runs as a demo without a license: complete documents can be read and printed for free, editing works on the first 3 pages, saved files carry a "DEMO" watermark, and password protection and Word/Excel/PowerPoint/JPG/EPUB export are disabled. To unlock the full version, click "Buy Full Version" in the app and pay through the Microsoft Store (one-time, no subscription). A license key bought at https://mkbsoft.net also works via the "Enter License" button; a corporate license for up to 5 PCs ($29.99 one-time) is available there. PDF Editor Pro is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license; source code: https://github.com/mkb-fcb/pdf-editor-pro
Kindle is a trademark of Amazon.com, Inc. PDF Editor Pro is not affiliated with Amazon.

--- What's new in this version ---
• Scanned PDFs can now be edited: change, delete or move text on scans and photocopies. New words are drawn in the scan's own typeface, size, weight and tone, also on coloured paper and for white text on coloured bars.
• Built-in text recognition (OCR) for scanned pages: two engines work together and read small, faint and dot-matrix print and accented characters accurately.
• New "Clean Background" (Edit tab): turns the grey copier tone, yellowed or coloured paper and shadows of scanned pages white. Keep the colours or make it black and white; form boxes are kept.
• Scanned documents export to Word as editable text laid out like the original page; Excel, text files and "Copy Text" use the recognised text too.
• Large scans (600 dpi) open, edit and export faster and use far less memory.

This is a desktop (Win32) application packaged as MSIX. It requires the runFullTrust capability because it is a classic desktop app (Python + WebView2) that opens and saves PDF files chosen by the user anywhere on disk.

Without a license the app runs as a free demo (full reading and printing, editing limited to the first 3 pages, watermark on saved files, some exports disabled). To test the full version, please use this license key (valid for up to 10 PCs):

    License key: BILIR-G5SP-XCRG-KDKM

Steps:
1. Launch "PDF Editor Pro".
2. The editor opens in demo mode with an orange banner at the top. Click "Lisans Gir" (Enter License) in that banner. The license screen follows the interface language.
3. Enter the key above and click "Aktivasyonu Başlat" / "Activate". An internet connection is needed for this one-time activation. The banner disappears and the full version is unlocked.
4. Click the open button and choose any PDF file to test editing, merging, splitting, compressing, exporting and password protection.

The interface opens in Turkish by default. To switch to English, click the "TR" language button in the toolbar and choose "English".
The full version can also be bought in-app through the Microsoft Store add-on "pdfeditorpro_full" ("Buy Full Version" button in the demo banner). License keys from https://mkbsoft.net keep working as an alternative, as permitted for non-game apps under Microsoft Store Policy 10.8.

Scanned PDFs: this version recognises the text of scanned pages so it can be edited. For this the package includes Tesseract OCR (Apache License 2.0, folder _internal	esseract), started by the app as a helper process. It runs entirely on the PC; no document or text is sent anywhere. To try it, open any scanned PDF (a page that is one picture), wait for the "Reading the text of the scanned page…" note to disappear, then click a line of text.
