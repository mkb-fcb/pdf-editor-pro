--- Product name ---
PDF Editor Pro - MKBSoft

--- Description ---
PDF Editor Pro is a fast, lightweight PDF editor for Windows. Everything runs locally on your PC — your documents are never uploaded to the cloud.

EDIT
• Edit, move and delete existing text directly on the page
• Add new text, images, rectangles and circles
• Highlight content, add stamps and watermarks
• Erase selected areas — the underlying text is permanently removed

ORGANIZE
• Merge multiple PDFs into one
• Split by page range or extract pages
• Rotate, reorder, insert and delete pages with thumbnail view

CONVERT & PROTECT
• Export to Word (DOCX), images, plain text and e-book (EPUB) — and send to Kindle
• Compress PDFs with selectable quality to reduce file size
• Add or remove password protection

Multilingual interface: English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Russian.

FREE DEMO & FULL VERSION

To unlock the full version, buy a license key at https://mkbsoft.net and enter it via the "Enter License" button. Individual license: $19 one-time for 1 PC. Corporate license: $80 one-time for up to 5 PCs. No subscription.

--- What's new in this version ---
• PDF to e-book: convert PDFs to EPUB with reflowing text, headings, chapters, images, charts and tables.
• Send to Kindle: copies straight to a Kindle connected by USB, or sends the e-book with your email program or Amazon's web page.
• AZW3 export for Kindle when the free Calibre app is installed.
• Fixes for the free demo installer.

Without a license the app runs as a free demo (max 3 pages, watermark on saved files, some exports disabled). To test the full version, please use this license key (valid for up to 10 PCs):

    License key: BILIR-G5SP-XCRG-KDKM

Steps:
1. Launch "PDF Editor Pro".
2. The editor opens in demo mode with an orange banner at the top. Click "Lisans Gir" (Enter License) in that banner.
3. Enter the key above and click "Aktivasyonu Başlat" (Start Activation). An internet connection is needed for this one-time activation. The banner disappears and the full version is unlocked.
4. Click the open button and choose any PDF file to test editing, merging, splitting, compressing, exporting and password protection.

The interface opens in Turkish by default. To switch to English, click the "TR" language button in the toolbar and choose "English".
